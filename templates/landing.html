How it works

Three steps to a living tree

  1. Plant your tree

    Add yourself, your parents and the grandparents you remember. “About 1921” is fine.

  2. Invite family

    Everyone adds what they know: dates, stories, the photos in the shoebox.

  3. Watch it grow

    Play the time-lapse at the next family dinner and see who asks for a copy.

Private by default

Only the people you invite can see your tree

Living relatives' birthdays and birthplaces stay hidden from view-only guests, because those are the answers to bank security questions. Other families can only find your ancestors if you switch that on, and only people who have passed away are ever matched.
