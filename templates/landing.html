Made for the people in it

Everything your family needs to gather its story in one place, and nothing that gets in the way.

  1. 01

    A tree you can walk around

    Spin, zoom and fly between generations. Every relative hangs in a brass medallion with their photo or initials.

  2. 02

    Watch your family grow

    Press play and a century unfolds. People appear in the year they were born, and leaves turn gold for those who've passed.

  3. 03

    How are we related?

    Pick any two people and Kinroot names it, from great-aunt to second cousin once removed, then lights the path between you.

  4. 04

    Invite the whole family

    Send a link. Relatives claim their own leaf and add what they know, and you decide who can view and who can edit.

  5. 05

    Talk on the tree

    Found a relative? Send a request and message once they accept. Nobody can message you out of the blue.

  6. 06

    Bring your research

    Import a GEDCOM file and export yours any time. Rough dates like “about 1921” are fine, and nothing is ever locked in.

Private by default. Only the people you invite can see your tree.

Living relatives' birthdays and birthplaces stay hidden from view-only guests, because those are the answers to bank security questions. Other families can only find your ancestors if you switch that on, and only people who have passed away are ever matched.
